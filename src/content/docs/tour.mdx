      <h3>On your phone too.</h3>
      <p>At phone width the dock becomes a bar and the Assistant slides up from the bottom. Ask for a still on the couch, press Generate, and find it in the Gallery later.</p>

        <li>Remembers what you like, and lets you edit what it remembers</li>

      <p class="hs-eyebrow">Create</p>
      <h2>One screen for every image, clip and voice.</h2>
      <p>Pick a model, write a prompt, make takes. Switch between image, video and audio without losing your prompt.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Presets from Draft to Final, with time and peak memory before you run</li>
        <li>Call characters and places by name with @</li>
        <li>A live preview while it samples</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/studio/create/">Read the Create guide →</a>
    </div>
    <Shot src="create" alt="Create with Qwen Image: live preview at step 12 of 40, presets, two references, canvas, quality and a LoRA" />
  </article>

  <article class="not-content hs-room hs-flip">
    <div class="hs-copy">
      <p class="hs-eyebrow">Video</p>
      <h2>Clips with sound, from a line of text.</h2>
      <p>LTX-2.5 and MiniMax H3 make video with matching sound. Animate a still, extend a clip, or retake a few seconds of it.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Fast, Quality and Low memory presets</li>
        <li>Length from the prompt, or set by hand</li>
        <li>Pin images at chosen times as keyframes</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/models/ltx/">Read about LTX-2.5 →</a>
    </div>
    <Shot src="create-video" alt="Create with LTX-2.5 text to video: an airship over cliffs at sunset in the preview, with presets, canvas, length and quality settings" />
  </article>

  <article class="not-content hs-room">
    <div class="hs-copy">
      <p class="hs-eyebrow">Graph</p>
      <h2>The same workflow, as nodes.</h2>
      <p>Every workflow is a small graph of typed steps. Flip from the form to the graph to see how a run is wired. Editing one edits the other.</p>
      <a href="/studio/create/">How Studio and Graph views work →</a>
    </div>
    <Shot src="graph" alt="Graph view of Qwen Image: prompt, encode, model, sample and decode nodes wired together, with the sample node's settings" />
  </article>

  <article class="not-content hs-room hs-flip">
    <div class="hs-copy">
      <p class="hs-eyebrow">Gallery</p>
      <h2>Every take remembers how it was made.</h2>
      <p>Prompt, model, seed, size and inputs travel with each take. Send any of them back to work in one click.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Lineage: what a take was made from and where it was used</li>
        <li>Use in… Animate, Edit, Extend or Retake</li>
        <li>Save as an element, a look or a pipeline</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/studio/gallery/">Read the Gallery guide →</a>
    </div>
    <Shot src="gallery" alt="Gallery grid of takes with filters, and the side panel showing how the selected take was made and its Use in actions" />
  </article>

  <article class="not-content hs-room">
    <div class="hs-copy">
      <p class="hs-eyebrow">Timeline</p>
      <h2>Cut the film where you make it.</h2>
      <p>Direct new shots in the gaps, retake a section, add voice lines, and export one MP4.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>A new shot can start from the last frame of the one before</li>
        <li>The sequence's cast and look apply to every shot</li>
        <li>Export copies streams when it can, re-encodes when it must</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/studio/timeline/">Read the Timeline guide →</a>
    </div>
    <Shot src="timeline" alt="Timeline with shots of a fox in the snow, a new shot being composed in a gap, and clip actions for Extend, Retake and Replace" />
  </article>

  <article class="not-content hs-room hs-flip">
    <div class="hs-copy">
      <p class="hs-eyebrow">Pipelines</p>
      <h2>Batches that check their own work.</h2>
      <p>Chain workflows and run them over a list while you are away. A judge looks at each take against your checklist and keeps the best.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Review gates where you pick, or agree with the judge</li>
        <li>It learns from the takes you keep and reject</li>
        <li>Every retry is capped and logged</li>
      </ul>
      <a href="/studio/pipelines/">Read the Pipelines guide →</a>
    </div>
    <Shot src="judge" alt="Judge review: the judge suggests take 3 as clean and sharp, with checklist chips and the pipeline's memory of past picks" />
  </article>
</div>
