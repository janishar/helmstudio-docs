Open **Settings** from the foot of the dock. Everything here is yours; the [Assistant](/studio/assistant/) can point
you here but never changes these for you.

## Account

Your **Name**, **Email** and **Roles**. Only the admin can change them, in [Admin › Users](/studio/admin/#users).

## Theme

Under **Appearance**, pick one of seven themes. It applies at once and is saved on this device.

- **Forest ink** (the default)
- **Deep ocean**
- **Aubergine dusk**
- **Espresso**
- **Oxblood**
- **Indigo night**
- **Parchment** (light)

Each card in the picker is painted in its own theme, so you can see it before you choose.

## Password

To change your password:

1. Enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter the **New password**: at least 10 characters, and not a common password.
3. Enter it again under **Confirm**.
4. Press **Change password**.

Changing it signs you out everywhere else.

## Sign out

Press **Sign out**.
