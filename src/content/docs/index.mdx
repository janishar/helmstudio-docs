<section class="not-content hs-band" aria-labelledby="assistant-h">
  <p class="hs-eyebrow">The Assistant</p>
  <h2 id="assistant-h">A co-director that <span class="hs-grad">works the screen</span> with you.</h2>
  <p class="hs-lede">Say what you want in a sentence. The Assistant opens the right screen, picks the model, writes the prompt and sets the look. You watch every change land, with Undo on each one. It never spends GPU time without asking.</p>
  <ol class="hs-flow">
    <li><b>Ask</b><span>"Make a rainy London evening still of @mara, cinematic."</span></li>
    <li><b>It does the work</b><span>Opens Create, sets Qwen Image Turbo, writes the prompt, adds Blue hour. Each step can be undone.</span></li>
    <li><b>It asks first</b><span>A card shows the model, size, seeds, the host and the time it will take. You press Generate.</span></li>
    <li><b>Steer it live</b><span>Type while it works. "Use Turbo instead" is read at the next step, and it changes course.</span></li>
  </ol>
  <div class="hs-duo">
    <Shot src="assistant-steer" caption="Steer while it answers · Enter adds your words, Stop ends it" alt="Three moments of steering: the box stays open while it works, a sent message waits for the next step, then the Assistant switches to Qwen Image Turbo at 16:9" />
    <Shot src="assistant-timeline" caption="Script to shots · it looks at your takes and places new shots" alt="Timeline with the Assistant: it picked the sharpest of four takes, then turned a written scene into three new shots" />

<section class="not-content hs-section" aria-labelledby="machines-h">
  <p class="hs-eyebrow">Your machines</p>
  <h2 id="machines-h">Runs where your GPUs are.</h2>
  <p class="hs-lede">Each run goes to a machine with the weights and the memory, or to the one you pick. Nothing you make leaves your machines.</p>
  <div class="hs-specs">
    <div class="hs-spec"><small>Apple Silicon</small><b>MLX, PyTorch and native</b><span>Runners share one memory budget on each Mac.</span></div>
    <div class="hs-spec"><small>NVIDIA</small><b>One server or many</b><span>PyTorch and native runners, with smaller fp8 and int8 weights.</span></div>
    <div class="hs-spec"><small>Your team</small><b>Private per person</b><span>Each user sees only their own takes, sessions and pipelines.</span></div>
  </div>
</section>
